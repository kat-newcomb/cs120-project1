About the Band

Five friends, one van, and a lot of vinyl.

Our Story

Velvet Thunder started in 2014 as a one-night reunion of musicians who had played in Ohio bar bands for years. The phone kept ringing, so we kept playing. Now we play over 60 shows a year around central Ohio.

Meet the Band
